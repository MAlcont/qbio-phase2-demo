Phase 2 deliberation

GitHub Pages renders only the public aggregate state: who is currently winning, and—once discussion begins—how much eligible voting power is represented by explicit intentions. Python remains an Etherscan verification/render harness.<7n>

How this is supposed to work: this page is rendering the skeleton of the project and exposing all data, but in final implementation we will the following process. Phase 2 is supposed to be a community phase - without identity and some community rules, it's just a hurdle. 2) the DAO will send to this list of people a mail to sign up on its site and connect a 2fA device. The account will be linkable to one or more wallets. 2fA + Eth address should be simple to adopt for everyone, and address is actually only needed if you want your opinion to "weight" - you're still free to express it. 3) let's say a proposal passes the simplified Phase 1 the Gov wg is asking - it gets posted here then. This is supposed to be a proposal that has been developed after #community-suggestion talk on Discord, a thumbs up vote there again, and now anyone can post it in this "limbo". From this moment on, management of the proposal for Phase 2 and 3 could even be passed to the DAO account, but this is just a note. 4) how the "forum" on the DAO site is supposed to work (and bring traffic!). Everyone can comment the proposal. The site is obviously highly customisable if we'll some analytics. Having traffic on the site means that even lenghty discussions, between people that can't help but drag the conversation "to their own mill", will be a net positive for the DAO. The hurdle is of course registration, but again, Phase 2 isn't serious with just a 10k $qbio treshold. Community is made of real people! what happens with the analytics? The site renders immediately the opinions: a) by default, everyone starts at NO. This can be simply changed from the account, in the proposal page. Changing opinion is suggested everytime you comment. This way nothing can be rushed up to Commons for Phase 2 voting and "notifications" to stakeholders is automated... b) the site certificates opionion on its backend, not on-chain - so you don't "vote" through the wallet, just sign-in with it or 2fA - and shows only if either Yes/No is winning. It also gives a % of the explicit voting power - how many wallets haven't been dormant on the proposal. c) so: there are "certified" accounts, having received a mail from the DAO, that can either stay "absent" (dormant) or obtain a second certificate and express opinion/comment. There can also be anonymous accounts, or AI agents, with a different, reduced weight - for example automated to debate whether a proposal has realistic references. d) the moment a proposal is unambiguosly favoured - Yes is winning and explicit power is high enough to win - the DAO can really just automate both Commons and Snapshot vote, but this is secondary, it can stay manual. All of this is supposed to fix a few issues: discussion of proposals happen and helps the site have traffic, but governance itself is slowed down - longer phase 2, no passing until power is explicited; KYC: I don't know how anyone feel about the tokens treshold, I don't like having a facetous measure. If Phase 2 is supposed to protect the community, it's ok to force as many people as we can to "identify" - we already know most of each other currently; these - discussions, traffic, time and accountability are the 3/4 perks of this platform.
